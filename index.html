Matthew Casey

I graduated in 2025 from the University of Limerick with a degree in Music, Media & Performance Technology. Soon after I had the opportunity to work in the sound department on Going Dutch Season 2. Being part of a real production environment really reinforced my interest in the sound industry and confirmed that this is the area I want to continue working in. I genuinely enjoy working with audio and am keen to keep developing my skills, learn from others in the field, and contribute to creative projects across film, television, and digital media.

Recent Experience

Working on Going Dutch Season 2 gave me a clear understanding of basic on-set etiquette and how a sound team works within a production. It was a useful introduction to professional set environments and reinforced my interest in continuing to work in the sound industry.

On film sets, I've gained hands-on experience with the basic setup and teardown of sound equipment, including microphone placement and connections. I've also worked closely with video assets to make sure audio lines up properly with picture, helping everything run smoothly in post-production. Being part of a sound team taught me a lot about collaboration, clear communication, and staying focused under deadlines, and I really enjoy contributing to the creative process on set.

Beyond audio, I am interested in getting experience in other departments such as camera, art and video assist, to broaden my understanding of production as a whole.

Education

BSc in Music, Media & Performance Technology — University of Limerick (2021–2025)

At UL, I'm studied the comprehensive MMPT course, which covers all aspects of modern media production and technology. The program offers a huge, broad range of subjects from audio engineering and video production to digital storytelling and media theory.

For my final year project, I explored the cultural significance of the Yamaha DX7 synthesizer and how it revolutionized music forever. My research examined its groundbreaking FM synthesis technology, affordability that democratized music production, and enduring influence on genres from 80s pop to modern electronic music.
