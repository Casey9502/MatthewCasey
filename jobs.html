Sound Trainee – Going Dutch 2

My role as a sound trainee involved managing core audio systems on the set of *Going Dutch 2* to ensure flawless production continuity. I delivered essential on-set support through equipment maintenance, acoustic treatment, and the precise setup and teardown for every scene, while coordinating with other departments like camera to align technical workflows.

  • Managed and organized sound equipment inventory to ensure all gear was operational and ready for use.
  • Provided on-set technical support, troubleshooting cables, microphones, and wireless audio systems for the boom operator and sound mixer.
  • Implemented sound protocols, such as applying acoustic treatments, to ensure clean audio capture.
  • Oversaw battery management, charging cycles, and backup equipment checks to prevent failures during filming.
  • Coordinated directly with other departments, including Video Assist and Camera, to ensure microphone placement was clear of frame and to synchronize timecode for efficient post-production workflow.
